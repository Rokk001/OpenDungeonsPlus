# Worker jobs

A keeper can assign workers to six jobs: digging, claiming ground, claiming and reinforcing walls, carrying, cleaning and reloading traps. The fixed assignment for each job starts at zero. The free-worker mode starts as Automatic; the initial priority order is carry, dig, claim ground, claim wall, clean, reload. The initial carry order is own knocked-out creatures, enemy knocked-out creatures, corpses, gold, item bags and herb bundles, gift boxes, item boxes and mission stones, then crafted traps and doors. Gold first starts off.

Fixed workers are selected by their worker skill for that job, then their level and name. Cleaning and reloading use level and name. They stay on their assigned job and wait when it has no work. If too few workers remain, their fixed places are shared in the proportions requested, rounding by largest remainder in the order above. New workers fill missing places. Workers in the hand, knocked out, possessed, fighting, fleeing or defending the heart do not participate until they return, and retain their job meanwhile.

The keeper's reachable work is counted each turn. Beyond a worker's sight, work must be on the keeper's own or unclaimed ground; within sight the existing rules for other ground also apply. Digging counts the available places by each open face, ground and wall claiming count suitable tiles, carrying counts things with an accepting destination, cleaning counts piles, dirty corridors and room cleaning places, and reloading counts affordable unloaded traps. By default, a room has at most one cleaner per started 15 tiles. Work already taken by a worker still counts.

Automatic spreads free workers as evenly as possible across jobs with work. Priority list uses the configured order and parts 6:5:4:3:2:1; jobs without work are skipped and surplus goes to the remaining jobs. No job takes more free workers than its work supports after fixed places. A free worker keeps its job while its share allows it; otherwise it picks the job with the largest unfilled share. Workers without suitable work wait. A worker finishes its current target before changing job.

Workers spread over dig places, claim walls at random and respect the keeper's carry order. Gold first makes diggers prefer marked gold and gem walls. A worker dropped by hand follows the dropped task once, then returns to its assigned job. Own and enemy knocked-out creatures and corpses take priority in the default carry order; allied knocked-out creatures are left alone.
