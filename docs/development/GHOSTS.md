# Ghosts and ghost hunters

A fallen non-worker leaves a ghost and a corpse. Ghosts float across open terrain and through doors, scare timid creatures, and eventually dissolve. Workers vanish when killed.

A researched ghost room needs at least a joined 4x4 block. Hunters take backpacks from its hooks, catch ghosts on their keeper's territory, and empty their boxes into cauldrons. Ghost levels fill the keeper's shared ectoplasm bar. A soul crystal costs two ectoplasm and falls onto a free floor tile of the room; bell, hooks and gauges stand on the walls, or on racks along the room edge when no wall is adjacent. Its controls choose research points, mana or soul crystals, and whether hunters revive own creatures whose bodies remain in an own crypt. Enemy ghosts reveal part of their owner's territory.

Corpses lie for two minutes and decay for four minutes; carrying pauses their clocks. The crypt consumes fully decayed bodies in groups to raise liches. Soul crystals raise a creature below level 30 by one level; creatures decide whether to buy and use them from their wage and current experience. Merchant purchases and sales pay the keeper's margin.

Ghost lifetime, corpse phases, boxes, hunts, crypt contents, ectoplasm and keeper controls persist in saves. Network peers check their wire schema before loading game records.
