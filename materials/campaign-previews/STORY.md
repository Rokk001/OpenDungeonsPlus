# Campaign story outline

Status: draft for Mario's approval; story text changes and preview generation remain paused.

## Red thread

Master, your first claim beneath Mossgate is the opening move in a campaign to take High King Castellan's throne at Hollowmark Citadel.
The same adviser accompanies you throughout, calling you Master or my lord as village resistance grows into the kingdom's organised defence.
Each fallen warden exposes another barrier to your advance: guarded crossings, fleeing commanders, rival dungeon hearts and finally the royal court.
Hidden stones carried home from Tinmoor, Hollin Fen, Lanternhill and Highcairn reveal optional trials whose rewards restore the Heartstone and unlock Summon Champion when complete.
The Heartstone is a parallel source of strength, never a prerequisite invented for the main campaign, and neither alternative province route is treated as a second conquest.
After Varn's Crossing, an optional night of siege can send your strongest surviving fighter onward, while Silverdeep and Wraithwood strip away the last rival powers before Kingsfall.
Prince Aurel's defeat and the capture or conversion of his three champions bring the struggle to Castellan's three courts, where the king's death fulfils the promise made at Mossgate.

## Confirmed source decisions

Mario confirmed that four Heartstone fragments, The Swarm Night's fighter carry-over reward and The Skittle Cavern's actually trigger-spawned guards match the current gameplay and are correct for this outline. The Skittle Cavern preview may therefore depict its spawned guards alongside a placed worker; no fifth fragment, changed reward or invented warden body is introduced.

## Source guardrails

All 31 referenced level files were read for objectives, victory triggers and scripted messages alongside the campaign configuration. The adviser and the throne objective are the proposed connecting narration; wardens, rewards, level objectives and unlock conditions remain those of the current source. No claim is made that named wardens with no placed body must be killed. No bonus trial is made compulsory. No resources or creatures are promised to persist between levels except The Swarm Night's existing fighter reward. Ashcombe's escape road, Dunmarrow's escape shaft, Kingsfall's champions and Hollowmark's gate routes follow the actual level triggers rather than simplifying them into conflicting objectives.
